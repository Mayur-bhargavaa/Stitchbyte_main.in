## Patterns across all six
1. **AI is now the lead message.** Four of six lead with AI in the headline. A generic "web development company" headline is no longer distinctive.
2. **Nobody shows prices.** None publish pricing. Stitchbyte's published prebuilt prices (e.g. ₹45,000) are unusual and a real differentiator for cost-conscious buyers.
3. **Named free offers beat "Contact Us".** PixelCrayons and IndiaNIC lead with a specific free review or evaluation. Stitchbyte's three audits follow this pattern.
4. **Proof is numeric and badge-heavy** (Clutch, GoodFirms, Google Partner, project counts). Several figures are inconsistent on their own sites (Acodez: "600 customers" vs "1000+ projects"; IndiaNIC: "17 industries" with 20 listed), so reader trust in these is shaky.
5. **Forms ask for a lot or are hidden.** Unified asks for six fields plus a captcha and file upload. Several others route to a contact page with no visible form.
6. **WhatsApp is rare.** Only Future Profilez and Acodez show it, as a plain link.

## Gaps Stitchbyte can use
- **Ready-to-buy products with visible prices and live demos.** No competitor offers this. Stitchbyte's `/prebuilt` pages already do.
- **WhatsApp-first contact.** Most competitors treat it as an afterthought.
- **Short, specific audits** (website, automation, Meta Ads) with a low-friction form.
- **Honest proof.** Competitors' numbers are hard to trust. Real named case studies with verified results will stand out.
- **Local focus.** Future Profilez is the only other Jaipur firm here and leads with global AI work, not local business enquiries.

## Do not copy
- Badge walls and big project counts we cannot substantiate.
- Long, multi-field forms with captcha.
