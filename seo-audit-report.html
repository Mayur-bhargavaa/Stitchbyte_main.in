Executive Summary

Top 5 Critical Issues 🔴

  1. ZERO Structured Data (JSON-LD) — No Organization, WebSite, Service, or any schema markup. Google cannot generate rich results.
  2. No metadataBase in root layout — OpenGraph/Twitter images resolve to relative URLs, breaking social sharing everywhere.
  3. No canonical URLs — No canonical tags on any page. Risk of duplicate content issues.
  4. Homepage is entirely client-rendered ("use client") — Critical SEO content requires JavaScript. AI crawlers cannot read this.
  5. No llms.txt file — AI search engines have no structured guidance for crawling.

Top 5 Quick Wins ✅
